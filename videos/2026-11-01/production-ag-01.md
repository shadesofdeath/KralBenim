## Writer — ag-01-dannebroge-copenhagen

### Verified facts (with sources)
- Dannebroge: Danish blockship and flagship of Commodore Olfert Fischer in the defense line, Battle of Copenhagen, 2 April 1801; hit by British fire incl. incendiary shells, caught fire, drifted north and exploded in the afternoon (museum: ~4 to 4:30 p.m.); blast heard across the city. Built 1772 (dendro dates match). ~357 aboard; 53 killed aboard, 19 missing. — Viking Ship Museum https://www.vikingeskibsmuseet.dk/en/news/flagship-dannebroge-found-225-years-after-battle-of-copenhagen ; HeritageDaily https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/04/wreck-of-danish-flagship-discovered-225-years-after-battle-of-copenhagen/157655
- Wreck found in Copenhagen harbor (King's Deep) ~15 m down, almost zero visibility, during archaeology required before the Lynetteholm artificial island is built; announced 2 April 2026. Finds: timbers matching the drawings, two cannons, cannonballs and bar shot, shoes, clothing, clay pipes, insignia, a human lower jaw. — same + AP/Inquirer https://www.inquirer.com/news/nation-world/denmark-archaeologists-warship-nelson-copenhagen-dannebroge-lynetteholm-20260402.html ("a construction project will soon cover the site")
- Nelson ignored Parker's signal to leave off action; reportedly put the telescope to his blind eye: "I really do not see the signal." Story told by contemporaries, treated as partly staged → narrated as "reportedly". Phrase "turn a blind eye" believed to come from it → "it goes back to". — Royal Museums Greenwich https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-12021 ; History Today https://www.historytoday.com/archive/battle-copenhagen
- Avoided: claiming Nelson's choice caused the Dannebroge's fire (she was burning before the signal); claiming the remains stay buried (finds are being recovered); eyepatch myth (Nelson is drawn without one).
