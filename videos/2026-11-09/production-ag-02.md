### Facts (verified)
- Jascon-4 capsized ~04:30–04:50 on 26 May 2013 in heavy Atlantic swell ~30 km off Nigeria, sank upside down ~30 m
  (Reuters via Maritime Executive; Irish Times "about 100 feet"; AP/KSL ~100 ft / 30 m).
- 12 on board; Okene (29, cook) the only survivor (Reuters: 10 bodies recovered, 1 missing).
- More than 60 hours in an air pocket (Reuters "more than 60 hours", "two and a half days"); pitch dark, his torches died
  within a day (AP); water around him, held on to the overturned washbasin (Reuters).
- Divers came to recover bodies; DCN Diving project manager: the diver went to grab the hand and "the hand grabbed him" (Irish Times).
- ~60 hours in a decompression chamber afterwards (Reuters, AP) — in the description only.
- Dropped: the Coke bottle (AP/Irish Times say one bottle; Reuters says no food or drink), exact water temperature/level, diver names.
