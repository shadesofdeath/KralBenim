## Facts (verified by the writer)
- Copper skin "3/32 of an inch, the thickness of two pennies placed together" (= 2.4 mm); copper 176,000 lbs (NPS, Statue Statistics).
- "The entire Statue was completed and assembled in Paris between 1881 and 1884", Eiffel's iron framework, shipped
  aboard the French Navy ship Isere, arrived in New York Harbor 17 June 1885, unveiled 28 October 1886 (NPS, Creating the Statue).
- Eiffel, "just prior to creating his famed Tower, was engaged to design the massive iron pylon"; "reduced to 350
  individual pieces and packed in 214 crates" (statueofliberty.org Overview + History; Smithsonian: 350 pieces / 214 crates / Isere / 17 June 1885).
- Eiffel Tower completed 1889 (kicker "Tower 1889"). Not used: Bartholdi's name (budget), weights.
