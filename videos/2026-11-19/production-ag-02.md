### Verified facts
- 30 January 1962, mission-run boarding school for girls at Kashasha, ~25 miles from Bukoba (west shore of Lake Victoria),
  three pupils first: Rankin & Philip 1963 as quoted in Hempelmann 2007 (*Humor* 20-1) + Wikipedia + Now I Know.
- 95 of 159 pupils affected 30 Jan–18 March 1962 (≈ 7 weeks → "within weeks"), school closed 18 March: Hempelmann + Wikipedia.
- Attacks of laughing and crying lasting a few hours (some up to 16 days): Hempelmann + Wikipedia.
- Tests for food poisoning / toxic substances negative, no infection found → mass hysteria / mass psychogenic illness:
  Hempelmann (Rankin & Philip) + Now I Know + Wikipedia.
- Spread to villages (Nshamba, 217 people, from late March) and other schools after the girls went home: Hempelmann + Wikipedia.
- Left out (sources disagree or single-sourced): "about 1,000 people", "14 schools", "18 months". No deaths are reported; not claimed.
