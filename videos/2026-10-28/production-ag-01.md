## Facts (verified)
- 15 June 1859: American settler Lyman Cutlar (also spelled Cutler) shot a Hudson's Bay Company pig rooting in his
  potato/vegetable patch on San Juan Island (NPS; Canadian Encyclopedia).
- Both the US and Britain claimed the San Juan Islands: the 1846 treaty's "middle of the channel" was read as Haro
  Strait (US) or Rosario Strait (Britain) (NPS).
- By 31 August 1859: 461 Americans camped with 14 field cannons + 8 naval 32-pounders (NPS); Canadian Encyclopedia:
  "more than 400" by mid-August. British warships Tribune (31 guns), Satellite (21), Plumper (10) (NPS).
- Rear Admiral R. Lambert Baynes would not "involve two great nations in a war over a squabble about a pig" (NPS; CE).
- Joint military occupation ~12 years; Kaiser Wilhelm I's commission ruled for the US on 21 Oct 1872 (NPS).
- The only casualty of the Pig War was the pig (NPS).
