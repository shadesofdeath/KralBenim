### Narration (one flowing text, written before any visuals; 443 chars)
This bird flew eleven days without landing. In October 2022, a godwit just five months old left Alaska over the Pacific.
No food, no water, no rest, because a godwit that lands on the sea dies. So how? Before takeoff, it got hugely fat. Its stomach
and liver shrank to make room. More than 8,400 miles later, it dropped onto a beach in Tasmania, nearly half its weight gone.
Its first trip ever was a world record. Subscribe for one true story a day.
