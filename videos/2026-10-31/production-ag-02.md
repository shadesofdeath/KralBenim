## Facts (verified by the writer)
- Team of five landed on Wrangel Island on 15 September 1921: Allan Crawford, Lorne Knight, Fred Maurer, Milton Galle +
  Ada Blackjack (hired as cook and seamstress), plus the expedition cat Vic/Victoria (Wikipedia; Atlas Obscura; History.com).
- January 1923 (Wikipedia: 28 Jan): Crawford, Maurer and Galle set out across the frozen sea ice towards Siberia for help;
  never seen again (History.com, Wikipedia).
- Knight, sick with scurvy, was nursed by Ada and died 23 June 1923 (History.com, Wikipedia).
- Alone, she taught herself to trap foxes and shoot birds (History.com); built a platform above her shelter to spot polar
  bears, had "a deep-rooted fear of polar bears" (Atlas Obscura; History.com).
- Rescue: schooner Donaldson, August 1923 (Atlas Obscura: 20 Aug; Wikipedia: 19 Aug → script says only "1923"/"Aug 1923").
- Not used: exact days alone (sources disagree), her son Bennett (budget), "could not swim" (not confirmed).
